About SheCare_

SheCare_ aims to reduce female unemployment in South Africa by connecting women to safe job opportunities.

It also empowers experienced grannies to share childcare knowledge with the new generation.

Please note: Only South African females aged 18 and above may register.

Your personal details are protected and only shared with verified, legal employers.

Feel free to contact us with any queries, and please let us know when you have secured a job so we can ensure you are safe.
